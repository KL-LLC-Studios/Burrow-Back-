Burrow support.

Found a bug, missing a purchase, or need help with your progress? Contact the developer directly.

Email support

kwonlanllc@gmail.com

Help us find the problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game version shown in Settings, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot or the chapter and round can help. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or an entire Apple receipt unless the relevant information is needed and unrelated personal details are removed.

The game is slow or will not open

Close and reopen the app, then check for an available update. If the game opens, try a lower graphics quality in Settings. Export a save backup before reinstalling: deleting the app can remove local progress. If the problem continues, email us with your device and game version.

I bought gems but they have not appeared

Reopen the game and visit Shop → Retry store with an internet connection. Purchases awaiting Apple approval deliver only after approval. Avoid buying the same pack again while delivery is waiting. If needed, email the product name, approximate purchase time and Apple transaction or order reference, with unrelated personal details removed.

Gem packs are consumable purchases. Restore Purchases does not recreate spent gems. Keep an up-to-date game backup or iCloud save for unspent gems and progress.

How do I protect or move my progress?

Use Settings → Save backups → Export save. Keep the exported file somewhere safe. If you use iCloud, save and load manually from Settings. Loading a save replaces the current progress and gem balance; export your current save first. Starting a new game clears progression but keeps unspent gems.

How do refunds work?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page for an eligible purchase. The developer cannot access your payment-card information or directly approve an Apple refund.

How do I request access to or deletion of support information?

Email kwonlanllc@gmail.com with “Burrow Back privacy request.” See the privacy choices section for local saves, iCloud data and information sent to support.

Support is provided by the solo developer. No fixed response time is promised.
