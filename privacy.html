Privacy policy.

Effective October 13, 2026 · Applies to Burrow Back for iPhone and this support website

This policy explains how the developer of Burrow Back (“we,” “us”) handles information when you play the game, use optional services, visit this website, or contact support. Privacy questions can be sent to kwonlanllc@gmail.com.

Playing on your device

The game stores progress and preferences on your device, including completed chapters, buildings, crew and skill upgrades, artifacts, costumes, currency balances, sound and graphics settings, and purchase-delivery transaction records. The game does not require a separate Burrow Back account.

The current release has no developer-operated gameplay analytics service. Frame and device-performance diagnostics used by the game stay on the device unless you choose to share a report or screenshot with support. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, health information or device location.

Optional iCloud saves and exported backups

When you choose to save to iCloud, the game sends your save file, chapter and save metadata to a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. The save includes progression, preferences, balances and gem transaction IDs used to avoid duplicate delivery. Apple provides the storage and account services. Cloud saving is manual; you can play without using it.

Export save creates a file through the iOS share interface. You choose where to store or send it. A backup may contain gameplay and purchase-delivery information, so share it only with recipients you trust. We receive a copy only if you send one to us. A load or import replaces your current game snapshot rather than combining balances from different saves.

App Store purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. We do not receive your full payment-card number, banking credentials or Apple Account password. The game uses StoreKit product information, verified transaction identifiers, product IDs, quantities and purchase status to deliver gems and check supported costume ownership. Gem balances and delivery records are kept with your save. Apple may separately maintain payment and transaction records under its own policies.

Advertising and tracking

Advertising is disabled in the release covered by this policy. The app’s Google Mobile Ads integration is not started and does not request ads in this configuration. We do not use this release to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If a future release enables advertising or introduces additional data collection, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures and provide any required choices before that change takes effect.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name your email includes, your message, and attachments you choose to provide. Messages may include device details, gameplay information or purchase references. We use this information to respond, investigate issues, protect against misuse and handle privacy requests. Support email is handled through Gmail and may be processed by our email provider to deliver and store the correspondence.

Do not include passwords, complete payment details or sensitive information unrelated to your request. Support correspondence is not used to build advertising profiles.

This website

This site uses static pages with no developer-added analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account system or contact form. Contact buttons open your email application. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the website under its own privacy statement. External links take you to services with their own policies.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by Apple for iCloud and App Store services, Google for support email, and the hosting provider for this website. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or protect users and the service. We do not provide support emails or saved-game files to data brokers.

Relevant provider policies: Apple, Google, and GitHub.

Retention and security

Local saves remain until you replace them, delete the app’s local data, or remove the app. Starting a new game retains unspent gems and purchase-delivery records. iCloud saves and files you export are separate copies and may remain after local data is removed. Apple and other providers apply their own retention rules.

We keep support messages for as long as reasonably needed to address the request and any related security, dispute or legal requirements. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Service providers may process information outside your state or country.

Your choices and requests

  • You can play without using iCloud saves and can export your progress from Settings.
  • Delete exported backups from the locations where you saved them. Removing the app can remove local progress and unspent gems; export a backup first if you want to keep them.
  • Manage iCloud data through your Apple Account and iCloud storage settings where available. Contact support if you need help locating the game’s cloud data. Deleting local data does not automatically delete an iCloud copy.
  • Email us to request access, correction or deletion of support information we hold. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent where it applies, or contact a privacy regulator. We may need to verify a request before acting and may retain information when legally required.

Children and family use

The game does not ask players to provide a birth date, create a social profile or chat with other players. In-game neighbor conversations are scripted and are not messages to real people. Parents or guardians can use Apple’s purchase controls and contact us about information a child may have sent to support. If we learn that a child has provided personal information that should not have been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Policy changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Changes to data practices will also be reflected in the app’s disclosures and choices where required.
